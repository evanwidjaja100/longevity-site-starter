# Usability Review Protocol

**Owner:** Product research
**Last reviewed:** 2026-07-28

## 1. Participants

Recruit 5–8 participants representing:

- Evidence-literate professionals (researchers, clinicians, science communicators)
- Non-specialist adults (no formal health or science training)
- Older adults (65+)
- Mobile-first users (primarily smartphone)
- Keyboard users (screen-reader or keyboard-only navigation)
- Users comparing a product purchase (active shopping intent)

## 2. Tasks

Each participant completes the following tasks in a moderated session. Record success/failure, time to completion, points of confusion, and verbatim comments.

| # | Task | Success criteria |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Explain what this publication does in your own words. | Accurate 3-sentence summary |
| 2 | Find guidance on improving sleep. | Reaches `/improve-sleep-before-buying-device/` or similar |
| 3 | Determine whether a specific health claim is strongly supported. | Locates evidence grade and rationale |
| 4 | Identify who reviewed a medical article. | Locates reviewer name and credentials |
| 5 | Find the limitations of a specific article. | Locates the `Limitations and uncertainty` section |
| 6 | Determine whether a product was actually tested by this publication. | Locates test record status and protocol version |
| 7 | Find a product's subscription and privacy information. | Locates buyer-facts section |
| 8 | Report an error in an article. | Navigates to `/corrections/` and identifies contact form |
| 9 | Find the publication's policy on AI-assisted work. | Locates AI-Assisted Work Disclosure page |
| 10 | Compare two products in the same category. | Uses ranking table and filters |

## 3. Evaluation dimensions

Rate each task on:

- **Success:** Completed independently (yes/partial/no)
- **Time:** Seconds to completion
- **Confusion:** Observed hesitation or incorrect navigation (none/minor/major)
- **Satisfaction:** Participant's self-rated ease (1–5 scale)

## 4. Testing environment

- **Desktop:** 1920×1080, Chrome/Edge
- **Tablet:** 768×1024, Safari/iPadOS
- **Mobile:** 375×667, Chrome/Android or Safari/iOS
- **Keyboard:** Desktop with mouse disconnected, Tab/Enter/Arrow navigation
- **Screen reader:** VoiceOver (macOS/iOS) or NVDA (Windows)

## 5. Documentation

For each session, record:

- Participant profile (role, device, browser, assistive tech)
- Task-by-task results
- Critical incidents (task failures, wrong paths, error messages)
- Verbatim quotes
- Recommendations

## 6. Analysis

After all sessions:

1. Calculate task success rates.
2. Identify the top 3 points of confusion across participants.
3. Prioritise fixes by severity (blocks task / slows task / cosmetic).
4. Create actionable recommendations for each issue.
5. Set a re-review date after fixes are deployed.

## 7. Privacy

- Do not collect names, email addresses, health histories, or other personal identifiers.
- Assign participant codes (P1, P2, etc.) for all records.
- Record screen activity only; do not record participant video or audio without explicit consent.
- Destroy raw session data after analysis is complete; retain only anonymised aggregate findings.
