# Launch Calendar

`launch-calendar.csv` is the single active launch sequence for Longevity Evidence Lab.

## Rules

- Files under `content/backlog/` are historical or ideation records, not approved publication commitments.
- A backlog date is not a publication date.
- "Testing required" content cannot advance without approved real-world records.
- Medical-review status is assigned and approved by humans only.
