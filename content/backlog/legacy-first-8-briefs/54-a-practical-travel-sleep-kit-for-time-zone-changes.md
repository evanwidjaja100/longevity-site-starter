# Brief 54: A Practical Travel Sleep Kit for Time-Zone Changes

- **Primary keyword:** travel sleep kit
- **Intent:** Commercial / buyer guide
- **Planned order:** 54
- **Medical review:** Yes
- **Hands-on testing:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a traveler assemble a practical sleep kit for time-zone changes, with medication content requiring review.

## Required sections
- Define travel sleep challenges: jet lag, unfamiliar environments, noise
- Create a kit framework: light, sound, comfort, timing aids
- Separate evidence-backed items from unnecessary gadgets
- Give a budget breakdown with essential vs nice-to-have categories
- List safety considerations and medication cautions

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Medical reviewer confirms medication content and safety boundaries.
- Testing record is approved with current model verification.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
