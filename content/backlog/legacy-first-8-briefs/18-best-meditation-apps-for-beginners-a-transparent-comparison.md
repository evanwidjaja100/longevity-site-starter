# Brief 18: Best Meditation Apps for Beginners: A Transparent Comparison

- **Primary keyword:** best meditation apps
- **Intent:** Commercial / tested comparison
- **Planned order:** 18
- **Medical review:** No
- **Hands-on testing:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a beginner choose a meditation app based on hands-on testing of core features, usability, and value—not marketing claims.

## Required sections
- Define beginner needs: guided sessions, duration, progress tracking, cost
- Create a comparison matrix: content library, voice styles, features, pricing, free tier
- Separate app quality from meditation tradition or philosophy
- Give a budget breakdown with clear value thresholds
- List limitations and what apps cannot address for mental health

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Testing record is approved with current pricing and test period disclosed.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
