# Brief 12: Cold Plunge Benefits, Risks, and a Safer Setup

- **Primary keyword:** cold plunge benefits
- **Intent:** Informational / safety guide
- **Planned order:** 12
- **Medical review:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a curious adult understand the actual evidence for cold exposure, recognize the risks, and set up a safer practice if they choose to try it.

## Required sections
- Distinguish anecdotal claims from research evidence
- Create a risk/benefit matrix with evidence grades
- Separate controlled exposure from extreme challenges
- Give a safer setup guide: temperature, duration, progression, contraindications
- List red flags and referral points for cardiovascular or circulatory conditions

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Medical reviewer confirms risk language and contraindication thresholds.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
