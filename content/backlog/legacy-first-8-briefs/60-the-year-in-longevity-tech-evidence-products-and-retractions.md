# Brief 60: The Year in Longevity Tech: Evidence, Products, and Retractions

- **Primary keyword:** longevity technology 2026
- **Intent:** Commercial / annual roundup
- **Planned order:** 60
- **Medical review:** Yes
- **Hands-on testing:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a reader understand the year's longevity technology developments, separating evidence from product claims and retractions.

## Required sections
- Define the scope: wearables, supplements, diagnostics, interventions covered this year
- Create a year-in-review framework: breakthroughs, products launched, retractions
- Separate evidence-backed developments from marketing hype
- Give guidance on evaluating longevity tech claims
- List red flags for overhyped or debunked interventions

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Medical reviewer confirms intervention boundaries and safety language.
- Testing record is approved with current model verification.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
