# Brief 26: NMN vs NR: Evidence, Regulation, and Buying Risks

- **Primary keyword:** NMN vs NR
- **Intent:** Commercial / evidence comparison
- **Planned order:** 26
- **Medical review:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a buyer distinguish NMN from NR in terms of evidence, regulatory status, and purchasing risks across different markets.

## Required sections
- Define NMN and NR and their metabolic pathways
- Create an evidence comparison: human studies, animal studies, bioavailability
- Separate regulatory status by jurisdiction (US, EU, others)
- Give a buying checklist with red flags and verification steps
- List safety considerations and research gaps

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Medical reviewer confirms safety language and research gaps.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
