# Brief 56: Anti-Aging Myths: What Current Evidence Does Not Support

- **Primary keyword:** anti aging myths
- **Intent:** Informational / myth review
- **Planned order:** 56
- **Medical review:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a reader identify common anti-aging myths and understand what current evidence does and does not support, using claim-by-claim evidence.

## Required sections
- Define "anti-aging" and why the term is problematic
- Create a myth-by-myth breakdown with evidence grades
- Separate evidence from marketing and social media claims
- Give guidance on what to focus on instead of debunked approaches
- List red flags for unsupported products and services

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Medical reviewer confirms myth boundaries and evidence grades.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
