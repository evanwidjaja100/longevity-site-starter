# Brief 52: Best Indoor Air Quality Monitors for Bedrooms and Offices

- **Primary keyword:** best air quality monitor
- **Intent:** Commercial / tested comparison
- **Planned order:** 52
- **Medical review:** No
- **Hands-on testing:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a buyer choose an indoor air quality monitor that accurately measures relevant pollutants for bedrooms and offices.

## Required sections
- Define air quality parameters: PM2.5, CO2, VOCs, humidity, temperature
- Create a comparison matrix: sensor accuracy, app quality, calibration, price
- Separate sensor capabilities from app/software features
- Give a budget breakdown with clear value thresholds
- List sensor limitations and calibration requirements

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
