# Brief 22: HRV Explained: What It Can—and Cannot—Tell You

- **Primary keyword:** heart rate variability explained
- **Intent:** Informational / evidence guide
- **Planned order:** 22
- **Medical review:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a reader understand what heart rate variability (HRV) measures, its evidence base for health and performance, and its significant limitations.

## Required sections
- Define HRV and the physiological mechanisms behind it
- Create an evidence summary: HRV for recovery, stress, training load, health prediction
- Separate validated uses from speculative interpretations
- Give guidance on consistent measurement and trend interpretation
- List red flags for diagnostic over-interpretation

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Medical reviewer confirms diagnostic boundaries and red-flag thresholds.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
