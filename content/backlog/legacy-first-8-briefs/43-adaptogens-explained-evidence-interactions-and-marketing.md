# Brief 43: Adaptogens Explained: Evidence, Interactions, and Marketing

- **Primary keyword:** adaptogens evidence
- **Intent:** Informational / evidence guide
- **Planned order:** 43
- **Medical review:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a reader understand what adaptogens are, review the evidence for common ones, and recognize marketing claims vs. research findings.

## Required sections
- Define adaptogens and the regulatory and scientific criteria
- Create an evidence summary by ingredient: ashwagandha, rhodiola, ginseng, others
- Separate evidence from marketing claims about "stress balance" and "energy"
- Give guidance on quality, dosing, and interaction screening
- List red flags for drug interactions and contraindications

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Medical reviewer confirms interaction warnings and contraindications.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
