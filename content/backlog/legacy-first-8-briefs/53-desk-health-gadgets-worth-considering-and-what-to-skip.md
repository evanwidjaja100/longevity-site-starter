# Brief 53: Desk Health Gadgets Worth Considering—and What to Skip

- **Primary keyword:** desk health gadgets
- **Intent:** Commercial / tested comparison
- **Planned order:** 53
- **Medical review:** Yes
- **Hands-on testing:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a buyer distinguish desk health gadgets that offer real value from those that are overhyped, based on hands-on testing and evidence.

## Required sections
- Define desk health categories: posture, movement, lighting, air quality
- Create a comparison matrix: effectiveness, build quality, price, space requirements
- Separate evidence-backed benefits from marketing claims
- Give a budget breakdown with clear value thresholds
- List safety considerations and what to skip entirely

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Medical reviewer confirms safety boundaries and medical-device claim avoidance.
- Testing record is approved with current model verification.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
