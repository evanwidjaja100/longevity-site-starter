# Brief 21: How Accurate Are Consumer Sleep Trackers?

- **Primary keyword:** sleep tracker accuracy
- **Intent:** Informational / evidence guide
- **Planned order:** 21
- **Medical review:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a buyer understand the accuracy limits of consumer sleep trackers compared to clinical polysomnography, so they can interpret data appropriately.

## Required sections
- Define sleep stage measurement and what polysomnography measures
- Create an accuracy comparison: stage detection, sleep/wake, duration, across populations
- Separate technical limitations from user interpretation errors
- Give guidance on when tracking is useful vs misleading
- List red flags for over-reliance on sleep scores

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Medical reviewer confirms clinical accuracy boundaries and red-flag thresholds.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
