# Brief 24: Creatine for Older Adults: Strength, Cognition, and Safety Evidence

- **Primary keyword:** creatine older adults
- **Intent:** Informational / evidence guide
- **Planned order:** 24
- **Medical review:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help an older adult understand the evidence for creatine supplementation for strength, cognition, and safety, including kidney and medication considerations.

## Required sections
- Define creatine and how it works physiologically
- Create an evidence summary: muscle mass/strength, cognitive function, bone health, safety
- Separate evidence in younger vs older adults
- Give dosing guidance with safety caveats and medication cautions
- List red flags for kidney disease, medication interactions, and when to consult a doctor

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Medical reviewer confirms kidney and medication cautions and safety boundaries.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
