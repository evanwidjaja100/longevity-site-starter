# Brief 14: A Practical Morning Routine for Energy Without Wellness Theater

- **Primary keyword:** healthy morning routine
- **Intent:** Informational / action guide
- **Planned order:** 14
- **Medical review:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a desk-bound adult adopt a simple, evidence-based morning routine that supports energy and circadian alignment without expensive gadgets or performative habits.

## Required sections
- Define "wellness theater" and why it matters for decision fatigue
- Create a 15-minute routine: light, movement, hydration, brief planning
- Separate evidence-backed practices from trendy additions
- Give a minimalist starter version and an expanded version
- List when to escalate to medical care for persistent fatigue

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Medical reviewer confirms fatigue escalation boundaries.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
