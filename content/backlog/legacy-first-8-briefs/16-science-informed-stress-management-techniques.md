# Brief 16: Science-Informed Stress Management Techniques

- **Primary keyword:** stress management techniques
- **Intent:** Informational / action guide
- **Planned order:** 16
- **Medical review:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help an adult experiencing everyday stress apply evidence-based techniques and recognize when professional care is needed.

## Required sections
- Define stress and distinguish acute from chronic stress
- Create a tiered toolkit: immediate (breathing), short-term (activity), long-term (routine)
- Separate evidence-backed techniques from unproven modalities
- Give a 7-day starter plan with time commitments
- List red flags and escalation points for severe or persistent symptoms

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Medical reviewer confirms escalation boundaries and mental health red flags.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
