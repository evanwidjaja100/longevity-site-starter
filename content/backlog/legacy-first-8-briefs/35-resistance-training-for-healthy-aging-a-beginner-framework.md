# Brief 35: Resistance Training for Healthy Aging: A Beginner Framework

- **Primary keyword:** resistance training longevity
- **Intent:** Informational / action guide
- **Planned order:** 35
- **Medical review:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a sedentary adult start resistance training safely with a beginner framework that includes clearance and progression caveats.

## Required sections
- Define resistance training and its benefits for healthy aging
- Create a beginner framework: frequency, intensity, exercises, progression
- Separate evidence-based practices from influencer routines
- Give a 4-week starter program with safety checkpoints
- List red flags for exercise safety and when to seek medical clearance

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Medical reviewer confirms exercise safety boundaries and clearance criteria.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
