# Brief 13: Red Light Therapy Devices: How to Compare Wavelength, Dose, and Claims

- **Primary keyword:** red light therapy device
- **Intent:** Commercial / buyer guide
- **Planned order:** 13
- **Medical review:** Yes
- **Hands-on testing:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a buyer evaluate red light therapy devices by comparing wavelength, dose, and manufacturer claims against independent measurements and research evidence.

## Required sections
- Define wavelength, irradiance, and dose in plain terms
- Create a comparison framework: wavelength bands, power density, treatment area, certification
- Separate clinical evidence from marketing claims for each wavelength
- Give a buyer checklist with red flags and verification steps
- List safety limits and contraindications for skin and eye exposure

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Medical reviewer confirms safety language and contraindications.
- Testing record is approved with current model verification.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
