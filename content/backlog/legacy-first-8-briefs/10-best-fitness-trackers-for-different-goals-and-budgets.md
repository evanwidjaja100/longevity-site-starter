# Brief 10: Best Fitness Trackers for Different Goals and Budgets

- **Primary keyword:** best fitness tracker
- **Intent:** Commercial / tested comparison
- **Planned order:** 10
- **Medical review:** No
- **Hands-on testing:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a buyer choose a fitness tracker that matches their specific goal and budget, based on hands-on testing rather than marketing claims.

## Required sections
- Define goal categories: general fitness, sleep, running, health metrics, budget
- Create a comparison matrix with tested accuracy, battery, comfort, app quality
- Separate hardware capabilities from software/service features
- Give a budget-tier breakdown with clear value thresholds
- List limitations and what trackers cannot measure reliably

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Testing record is approved with current firmware and model verification.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
