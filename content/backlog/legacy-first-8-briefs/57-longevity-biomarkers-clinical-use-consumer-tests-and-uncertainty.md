# Brief 57: Longevity Biomarkers: Clinical Use, Consumer Tests, and Uncertainty

- **Primary keyword:** longevity biomarkers
- **Intent:** Informational / evidence guide
- **Planned order:** 57
- **Medical review:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a reader understand longevity biomarkers, their clinical use, consumer test availability, and the significant uncertainty around them—without self-diagnosis.

## Required sections
- Define longevity biomarkers and how they differ from clinical biomarkers
- Create an evidence summary: which biomarkers have clinical backing vs speculation
- Separate clinical use from consumer test marketing claims
- Give guidance on interpreting biomarker trends responsibly
- List red flags for self-diagnosis and when to consult a doctor

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.
