# Brief 41: Alcohol and Sleep: Why Sedation Is Not Better Sleep

- **Primary keyword:** alcohol and sleep
- **Intent:** Informational / evidence guide
- **Planned order:** 41
- **Medical review:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a reader understand how alcohol affects sleep architecture, why sedation is not the same as restorative sleep, and how to manage consumption for better sleep.

## Required sections
- Define how alcohol affects sleep stages and architecture
- Create an evidence summary: alcohol's impact on sleep quality and next-day effects
- Separate evidence from cultural beliefs about "nightcap" benefits
- Give practical strategies for reducing alcohol's sleep impact
- List red flags for alcohol dependence and when to seek support

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.
