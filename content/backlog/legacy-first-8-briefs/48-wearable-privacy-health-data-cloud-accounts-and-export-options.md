# Brief 48: Wearable Privacy: Health Data, Cloud Accounts, and Export Options

- **Primary keyword:** wearable privacy
- **Intent:** Informational / privacy guide
- **Planned order:** 48
- **Medical review:** No

## Reader outcome
Help a reader understand what health data wearables collect, how it's stored and shared, and what export options exist—without legal conclusions.

## Required sections
- Define health data categories collected by wearables
- Create a privacy framework: data collection, cloud storage, sharing, retention
- Separate current policies from marketing claims about "privacy"
- Give guidance on reviewing current policies and privacy settings
- List red flags for data sharing and when to be concerned

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
