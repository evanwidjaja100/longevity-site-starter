- **Primary keyword:** protein older adults
- **Intent:** Informational / evidence guide
- **Planned order:** 34

## Reader outcome
Help an older adult understand protein needs, evidence-based targets, and practical meal planning—with kidney disease cautions included.

- Separate evidence-based targets from marketing "high-protein" claims
- Give practical meal ideas with protein content and timing guidance
- List red flags for kidney disease and when to consult a doctor

- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.
