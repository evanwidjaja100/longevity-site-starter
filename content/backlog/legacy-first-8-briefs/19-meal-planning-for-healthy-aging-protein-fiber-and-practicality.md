# Brief 19: Meal Planning for Healthy Aging: Protein, Fiber, and Practicality

- **Primary keyword:** healthy aging meal plan
- **Intent:** Informational / action guide
- **Planned order:** 19
- **Medical review:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a middle-aged or older adult adopt a practical meal-planning approach that supports protein intake, fiber, and nutritional adequacy without rigid prescriptions.

## Required sections
- Define protein and fiber needs for adults 50+ with population limits
- Create a weekly planning framework: batch prep, shopping list, flexibility
- Separate evidence-based targets from marketing "superfood" claims
- Give a 7-day sample plan with substitutions and cost considerations
- List when to consult a dietitian or doctor for medical nutrition therapy

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Medical reviewer confirms protein/fiber targets and kidney disease cautions.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
