# Brief 46: How to Read a Supplement Label Without Being Misled

- **Primary keyword:** how to read supplement label
- **Intent:** Informational / action guide
- **Planned order:** 46
- **Medical review:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a reader decode supplement labels, spot misleading claims, and understand what the numbers actually mean—including jurisdiction-specific rules.

## Required sections
- Define supplement label components: Supplement Facts, ingredients, claims
- Create a label-reading checklist: serving size, %DV, proprietary blends, disclaimers
- Separate FDA-regulated facts from structure/function claims
- Give guidance on verifying third-party testing and certifications
- List red flags for misleading marketing and when to be skeptical

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Medical reviewer confirms regulatory boundaries and claim distinctions.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
