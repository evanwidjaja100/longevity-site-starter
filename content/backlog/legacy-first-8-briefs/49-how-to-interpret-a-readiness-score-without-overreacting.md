# Brief 49: How to Interpret a Readiness Score Without Overreacting

- **Primary keyword:** readiness score meaning
- **Intent:** Informational / action guide
- **Planned order:** 49
- **Medical review:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a reader interpret readiness scores from wearables without overreacting, emphasizing trends over single data points.

## Required sections
- Define readiness scores and how they're calculated
- Create a framework for interpreting trends vs single-day readings
- Separate useful signals from noise and over-interpretation
- Give guidance on when to adjust activity and when to ignore the score
- List red flags for over-reliance and when to seek medical care

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.
