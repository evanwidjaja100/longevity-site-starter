# Brief 59: A 30-Day Sleep Reset: Measurement, Habits, and Review Points

- **Primary keyword:** 30 day sleep reset
- **Intent:** Informational / program guide
- **Planned order:** 59
- **Medical review:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a reader implement a 30-day sleep reset program with measurement, habit changes, and review points—without it being a treatment plan.

## Required sections
- Define sleep hygiene and the 30-day reset framework
- Create a day-by-day plan: measurement setup, habit introduction, weekly reviews
- Separate evidence-based practices from trendy sleep hacks
- Give guidance on tracking progress and adjusting course
- List red flags for sleep disorders and when to seek medical care

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Medical reviewer confirms sleep disorder red flags and escalation boundaries.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
