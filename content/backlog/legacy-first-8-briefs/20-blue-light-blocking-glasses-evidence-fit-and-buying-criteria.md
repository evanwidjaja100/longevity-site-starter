# Brief 20: Blue-Light-Blocking Glasses: Evidence, Fit, and Buying Criteria

- **Primary keyword:** blue light blocking glasses
- **Intent:** Commercial / buyer guide
- **Planned order:** 20
- **Medical review:** Yes
- **Hands-on testing:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a buyer evaluate blue-light-blocking glasses by separating light-reduction claims from sleep-quality evidence and assessing fit and comfort.

## Required sections
- Define blue light spectrum and what lenses actually filter
- Create a comparison framework: lens material, coating, frame fit, price
- Separate sleep-quality evidence from light-reduction measurements
- Give a buying checklist with red flags and verification steps
- List eye safety considerations and contraindications

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Medical reviewer confirms eye safety language and contraindications.
- Testing record is approved with current model verification.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
