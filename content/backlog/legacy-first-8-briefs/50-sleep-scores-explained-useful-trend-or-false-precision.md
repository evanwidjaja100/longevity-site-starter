# Brief 50: Sleep Scores Explained: Useful Trend or False Precision?

- **Primary keyword:** sleep score accuracy
- **Intent:** Informational / evidence guide
- **Planned order:** 50
- **Medical review:** Yes

## Reader outcome
Help a reader understand what sleep scores mean, their proprietary algorithms, and whether they're useful trends or false precision.

## Required sections
- Define sleep scores and how different companies calculate them
- Create a comparison of scoring methodologies and what they emphasize
- Separate useful trend signals from algorithmic noise
- Give guidance on when to track sleep scores and when to ignore them
- List red flags for over-reliance and anxiety around sleep data

## Evidence and originality requirements
- Use current official documentation for product and regulatory facts.
- Use primary research and current systematic reviews for health claims.
- Log every material claim and its evidence note.
- Add original testing, diagrams, interviews, or decision frameworks.
- State uncertainty, population limits, and conflicts before conclusions.

## Release gate
- Medical reviewer confirms non-diagnostic boundaries and anxiety red flags.
- Affiliate disclosure before the first monetized link.
- Editor approves non-sensational title/body consistency.
- Assign a 6- or 12-month recheck.
