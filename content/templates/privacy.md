# Privacy

## What we collect

- **Standard web logs.** Our server records IP address, browser user-agent, referring page, and request timestamp for operational and security purposes.
- **Contact form submissions.** If you contact us, the information you provide is used solely to respond to your inquiry.
- **Newsletter.** If you subscribe, we store your email address and subscription preferences.

## What we do not collect

- Health information.
- Location data beyond the geographic region implied by IP address.
- Browsing behaviour across third-party sites.

## Third-party services

- **Analytics.** We may use aggregated, privacy-preserving analytics. Individual session tracking is not performed.
- **Hosting.** Our hosting provider processes standard request data to deliver pages.

## Data retention

- Log data: retained for up to 30 days, then aggregated or deleted.
- Contact form submissions: retained until the matter is resolved, then deleted.
- Newsletter data: retained until you unsubscribe.

## Your rights

- Access: request a copy of data we hold about you.
- Deletion: request removal of your data.
- Correction: request correction of inaccurate data.

To exercise these rights, [contact us](/contact/).
