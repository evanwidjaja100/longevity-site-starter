# Editorial Policy

## Scope

This policy governs all content published by Longevity Evidence Lab, including evidence guides, Consumer Lab reviews, methodology pages, and supporting materials.

## Claim-level evidence

Every material health or performance claim published in our content must be registered in the internal claim registry before or at the time of publication. Each claim record includes:

- the exact claim text;
- the source and verification status;
- an evidence grade (A, B, C, D, or U);
- the population, intervention, comparator, and outcome;
- the evidence design; and
- a verification date and reviewer identity.

## Evidence grading

Grades are assigned conservatively:

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A | Strong — consistent evidence from well-designed studies |
| B | Moderate — generally consistent evidence, some limitations |
| C | Limited — insufficient or conflicting evidence |
| D | Mechanistic or anecdotal — plausible but untested in human trials |
| U | Unclear — cannot be assessed with available information |

Grades apply to individual claims, not entire articles. The overall article confidence reflects the most material claims.

## Corrections

Substantive errors are recorded in a public correction notice. Minor edits (grammar, clarity, formatting) are not separately listed. Readers may report potential errors via the [corrections page](/corrections/).

## Commercial relationships

Commercial relationships are disclosed per content item. They never determine scores, ranking order, or editorial conclusions.

## Review cycle

All content has a scheduled review date. Materially overdue content is flagged and may be temporarily removed until updated.
