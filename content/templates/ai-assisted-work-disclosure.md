# AI-Assisted Work Disclosure

## How we use AI

Artificial intelligence tools may be used in the production of content at Longevity Evidence Lab. Specifically, AI may assist with:

- topic ideation and outline development;
- source organisation and citation formatting;
- grammar, clarity, and mechanical editing;
- metadata generation (keywords, descriptions);
- internal-link suggestions;
- code generation for site functionality.

## What AI does not do

- **AI does not independently verify medical claims.** Every material claim is reviewed against its registered source by a named human.
- **AI does not approve safety language.** Human review is required for all material claims affecting consumer safety.
- **AI does not assign evidence grades.** Grades are assigned or reviewed by an accountable human.
- **AI does not publish content.** Publication requires human authorisation.
- **AI does not interpret private health information.**

## Accountability

A named human editor is responsible for each piece of content. AI-assisted output affecting a material claim is checked against the source registry before publication.

This policy is governed by our [AI-Assisted Work Policy](/editorial-policy/).
