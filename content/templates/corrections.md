# Corrections

## Our approach

We treat accuracy as a continuing obligation. When a substantive error is identified — whether reported by a reader or discovered internally — we investigate, correct, and log it publicly.

## What constitutes a substantive error

- A claim that contradicts the underlying source.
- A misstated statistic, data point, or study finding.
- An evidence grade that does not match the stated methodology.
- A factual error in product specifications, pricing, or availability.

## What is not separately logged

Minor edits for grammar, clarity, formatting, broken links, or routine updates (e.g., refreshed pricing) are not recorded in the correction log unless they affect a material claim.

## How to report an error

To report a potential error, use the [contact form](/contact/) and include the page URL and a description of the issue.
