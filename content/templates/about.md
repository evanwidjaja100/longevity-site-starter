# About

## What We Are

Longevity Evidence Lab is a consumer-health and product-testing publication. We evaluate products, practices, and interventions that claim to support healthy aging. Our work is evidence-led: every material claim is registered, sourced, graded, and reviewed before publication.

## What We Are Not

We are not a medical practice. We do not provide individual health advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Our content is educational and evaluative, not prescriptive.

## Our Principles

- **Claims come first** — every material statement is traceable to a verified source.
- **Uncertainty is disclosed** — confidence is scoped, limitations are explicit.
- **Independence matters** — commercial relationships never determine scores or conclusions.
- **Corrections are public** — errors are recorded transparently.
