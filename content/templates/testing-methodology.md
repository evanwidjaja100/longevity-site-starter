# Testing Methodology

## Scope

This page describes the general approach used for Consumer Lab testing. Individual review methodology pages contain protocol-specific details.

## What we test

Products sold to consumers that make claims about health, performance, or physiological measurement. Candidates are selected based on:

- demonstrated reader interest;
- availability of an evidence base for evaluation;
- feasibility of objective measurement.

## How we test

Each test has a registered protocol filed before testing begins. Protocols specify:

- the measurement device(s) and firmware version(s);
- the test procedure;
- the conditions under which measurements are taken;
- the sample size and rationale;
- the primary and secondary outcomes;
- the statistical approach.

## Scoring

Scores are assigned by category using the buyer-facts framework. Each category has defined criteria, weight, and a `minimum_meaningful_difference` below which differences are not reported as meaningful.

## Protocol deviations

Any deviation from the registered protocol is noted in the final report with a rationale.

## Transparency

All test results are published. Negative, null, and borderline results are reported with the same detail as positive results.
