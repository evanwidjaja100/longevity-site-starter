# Source Registry

## Purpose

The Source Registry is the public index of all external sources cited in material claims across Longevity Evidence Lab content. Every material claim in our published work is traceable to an entry here.

## Structure

Each source record includes:

- source title;
- author(s) or publishing organisation;
- publication date;
- URL or permanent identifier (DOI, PMID, etc.);
- date accessed;
- jurisdiction (publishing country or regulatory body);
- archive link (Internet Archive or equivalent);
- conflict notes, if any.

## How to use

The registry is organised by content item. Each content page lists its sources inline. The full searchable registry is available as a resource for readers who want to verify our sourcing independently.

## Inclusions

- Peer-reviewed studies cited in evidence guides and reviews.
- Manufacturer documentation and public specifications used in product evaluation.
- Government and regulatory sources.
- Grey literature where it supports a material claim.

## Exclusions

- Background reading that did not directly support a material claim.
- General reference works used for context but not cited as evidence for a specific claim.
