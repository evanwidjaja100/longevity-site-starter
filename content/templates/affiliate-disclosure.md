# Affiliate Disclosure

## How this site is funded

Longevity Evidence Lab is supported through a combination of reader support and affiliate commissions.

## What affiliate means

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click an affiliate link and make a purchase, we may receive a small commission at no extra cost to you.

## How it affects our content

- **No effect on scores.** Affiliate relationships never determine star ratings, ranking order, or editorial conclusions.
- **No effect on inclusion.** Products are selected for review based on reader interest and evidence availability, not commercial arrangements.
- **Disclosed per item.** Individual guides and reviews note applicable affiliate relationships in their commercial-disclosure area.

## Transparency

We do not accept payment for positive coverage. Sponsored content, if any, is clearly labelled as such and is subject to the same evidence standards as all other content.
