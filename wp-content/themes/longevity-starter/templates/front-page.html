How we evaluate evidence

Trust should be inspectable

Our trust signals reflect recorded editorial state. They are not decorative badges or substitutes for sources and limitations.

Evidence strength is shown

Grades include a plain-language rationale and an evidence cutoff date when recorded.

Uncertainty stays visible

Scope and limitations are presented alongside conclusions, not hidden at the bottom.

Review is scoped

Medical review appears only after assigned, authenticated completion and states what was reviewed.

Testing is documented

Tested-review presentation requires an approved, version-matched record and reproducible score.
