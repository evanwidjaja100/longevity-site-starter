# Usability Review Plan

## Objective

Run structured usability reviews with representative readers to validate that the publication is navigable, trustworthy, and understandable across different levels of health-literacy and device preference.

## Reader profiles

Test with at least one participant from each profile:

| Profile | Key characteristics | Recruiting source |
|---|---|---|
| **Evidence-literate professional** | Healthcare researcher, clinician, or science communicator. Comfortable with confidence intervals and study design. | Professional networks |
| **Non-specialist adult** | General reader without formal health-research training. May rely on intuition or testimonials. | Community outreach |
| **Older adult (65+)** | May have lower contrast sensitivity, reduced fine-motor control, and less familiarity with mobile patterns. | Senior centers, community groups |
| **Mobile-first user** | Primary device is a phone. May have limited data or older OS. | Social media, in-app recruitment |
| **Keyboard-only user** | Relies on keyboard navigation (no mouse). May use screen reader or magnification. | Accessibility communities |
| **Product comparison shopper** | Actively comparing a health/wellness product purchase. Needs buyer facts and testing data. | Existing newsletter subscribers |

## Test tasks

Ask each participant to perform the following tasks. Record success/failure, time to completion, points of confusion, and language issues.

| # | Task | Related KPI |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Explain what this publication does in your own words. | Communication clarity |
| 2 | Find the "Start Here" page and describe what you see. | Onboarding effectiveness |
| 3 | Navigate to a guide about sleep (or another topic). | Topic discovery |
| 4 | Determine whether a specific health claim on the page is strongly supported by evidence. | Evidence literacy |
| 5 | Find who reviewed an article and when. | Transparency / trust |
| 6 | Identify what the article's limitations are. | Uncertainty communication |
| 7 | Determine whether a specific product was actually tested by the publication. | Testing transparency |
| 8 | Find the subscription / mailing-list signup and privacy policy. | Privacy UX |
| 9 | Report an error or correction on a page. | Corrections workflow |
| 10 | Compare two products in the same category. | Comparison UX |

## Evaluation criteria

For each task, record:

- **Success**: Completed independently / Completed with hint / Failed
- **Time**: Seconds from start to completion (or abandonment)
- **Confusion**: Verbal or behavioral signs of uncertainty (record verbatim if possible)
- **Language issues**: Terms the participant did not understand (e.g., "evidence grade", "limitations", "confidence")
- **Accessibility barriers**: Focus loss, unlabeled elements, contrast issues, missing skip links

## Session format

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Duration | 30–45 minutes per session |
| Format | In-person or remote (screen-share) |
| Recording | Screen recording with audio (with written consent) |
| Moderator | Facilitator reads tasks aloud, does not guide |
| Note-taker | Second person records observations |
| Compensation | Gift card or equivalent per session |

## Privacy and ethics

- Do **not** collect private health histories, diagnoses, or medication lists.
- Do **not** record participant faces or identify them by name in published reports.
- Obtain written informed consent before each session.
- Store recordings securely, accessible only to the research team, and delete within 90 days.
- Participants may withdraw at any time.

## Deliverables

After completing sessions for all 6 profiles:

1. **Summary report**: Key findings, common failure points, and recommended fixes (prioritized)
2. **Issue log**: Per-task success rates, confusion points, and accessibility barriers
3. **Recommended changes**: Specific UI, content, or navigation improvements with estimated effort

## When to run

- **Initial baseline**: Before public launch (or as soon as 6 representative participants can be recruited)
- **Post-launch**: After 3 months of real usage data, or after any major navigation redesign
- **Ongoing**: Annual check-in with at least 3 profiles
